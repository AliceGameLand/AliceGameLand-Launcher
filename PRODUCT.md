# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players opening the Alice GameLand desktop launcher to join a Minecraft server. Returning players need their last server immediately. New players need a clear Microsoft sign-in step.

## Product Purpose

Select a server, prepare its client files, and launch Minecraft with the player's account and per-server settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The Electron renderer uses EJS and shared CSS. The distribution index supplies server order, icons and metadata. The existing account, updater, settings and launch paths own their state.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Alice GameLand name, existing mascot and original garden illustration. Show the complete illustration in the home content area. Use Korean by default and retain English strings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide keyboard operation, visible focus, text status labels and reduced-motion support.
